Power Music Admin Hub

An internal dashboard that turns incoming emails into trackable requests for the Power Music team.

What the app does

Power Music Admin Hub lets an administrator connect a Google Workspace or Gmail inbox. Once connected, the app reads new incoming emails in that inbox and creates a matching request in the dashboard, so the team can review, assign and follow up on each one.

How Google account access is used

An administrator chooses Connect Google Account in the admin settings and approves access on Google's consent screen. The app uses that access only to read incoming messages in the connected inbox and create requests from them. Administrators can disconnect an inbox at any time from the same settings page, or revoke access from their Google Account permissions page.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can use it

Access is limited to invited team members. The dashboard requires sign-in.
