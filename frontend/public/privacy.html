This policy explains what data Power Music Admin Hub ("the app") accesses, how it is used and stored, and how you can control it. The app is operated by REPLACE_WITH_COMPANY_NAME ("we", "us").

Data we access from your Google Account

When an administrator connects a Google account, the app requests permission to read email in the connected inbox (the Gmail gmail.readonly scope). The app accesses the sender, recipients, subject, date, body and attachments of incoming messages, and basic account identifiers such as the connected email address.

How we use this data

We use the data only to create and display requests in the dashboard from incoming emails, so that our team can review and act on them. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell Google user data. We do not share it with third parties, except service providers that host and operate the app on our behalf (hosting and database providers), and only as needed to run the app, or where required by law. We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless you give consent for specific messages, it is necessary for security purposes or to comply with law, or the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed database. Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored on the server side and are not exposed to the browser. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to the dashboard is restricted to authenticated team members.
